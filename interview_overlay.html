    Settings

    Checking…
    No notes yet? You can start with just your CV. Answers about your background and experience come from it, and Vault+AI adds general answers to technical questions.
    Gemini is required. Other vendors are optional.
    Gemini reads your notes, transcribes the interviewer and answers by default, so the app needs its key. Other vendors only add models to choose from. A key can come from an environment variable, which keeps nothing on disk and is the safer choice, or be entered here: for this run only, or remembered on this machine as plain text in api_keys.json.
    Keep follow-up questions on topic.
    Sends your recent questions and answers along, so a follow-up such as "walk me through the steps" stays on the same topic.
    A line to say while the answer loads.
    For high-stakes questions it cues you to scope the problem first. It is skipped for one-line factual answers.
    Flag questions worth scoping first.
    Scope it turns purple with a ◆ for troubleshooting, outage, recovery and design questions, but not for one-line factual or behavioural ones. It runs on this computer, with no API call.
    How long answers are.
    To the point: one sentence, expanded only if they follow up. Concise: a spoken paragraph. Balanced: specific bullets. Deep: a structured brief, set here only because it is not on the strip.
    Lead or hands-on framing.
    Lead: checkpoints and coordination, leaving out exact commands and vendor names unless asked. Hands-On: the commands and tools are the answer, for hands-on roles.
    Optional lens for a role-specific interview.
    Steers which of your real experiences lead and how they are framed. None changes nothing. It never invents anything, and grounding stays the same.
    Your notes only, or notes plus AI.
    Vault only: strictly your own notes, with an honest "no hands-on experience" when nothing matches. Vault + AI: your notes first, and general AI knowledge only when your notes have nothing. AI: general knowledge for everything, preferring your notes where they exist. In every mode an answer may not claim hands-on experience you don't have, and the badge shows which source was used.
    Silence that ends a spoken line.
    Higher tolerates thinking pauses without splitting one question into two (about 2000 to 2500 suits a considered interviewer). Lower finishes lines sooner. Takes effect the next time you start listening.
